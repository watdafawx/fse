Version: 0.7.0
Date: 2026-10-12
  Features:
    - Esc closes a window: window.create makes it the player's opened GUI when nothing else is, so Esc and E close
      it as they close the game's windows, and on_close hears it. esc = false for a window that stays open while the
      player uses the game's windows. A mod that closes its window on on_gui_closed itself still does (the library
      closes it a tick later, only when it is still there). theme.confirm and theme.menu take Esc for a moment and
      give it back to their window when they go (Esc, a second click on the gear, an answer).
    - theme.confirm{danger = true}: its yes button is red (the bad role, white text on our tiles), for what destroys or
      removes; yes / no take captions (verbs read best: "Remove", "Keep"), Yes / No by default.
    - Role ok_small: the main action's colours in a plain button's size and font (a form's Save, Add).
    - window.close(player, name): closes a window as its X does. window.reset_place(player, name): forgets its place
      and size.
    - Shared texts for every mod's windows (locale fstd.*): settings, docked (+ -tooltip), theme-button "Theme..."
      (+ -tooltip), reset-place "Reset size and place" (+ -tooltip), yes, no, confirm-title.
  Changes:
    - Toasts keep clear of every shown window on the screen (any mod's, and what sticks out of them; sizes read from
      the engine with the fse loader), trying below, above, right and left of their window, then the free spot nearest
      to it, then the top of the screen. A localised toast's height is guessed on the safe side, so the next mod's box
      doesn't cover a long one. On glass themes a toast (and a dock, a menu) is solid, readable over another window.
    - Esc closing a window: what is to close on the next tick is kept in storage (a player joining in between gets it
      too: no desync).
    - Lists: the game's dark row stripes are gone on our tiles (black bars on Frosted glass), unselected rows draw
      nothing over the list, and a list is the panels' inset colour (its text is the panels' ink), not a text field's.
    - Rail and folder tabs: the open one is in the accent with the accent's text (it was the window's colour: lost on
      Aquilo and CRT, pale on Vanilla).
    - Light themes: count-font labels (a count, SRQ's cost) are drawn without the game's black border.
    - Dark themes: slots and text fields have a 1 px outline in the dim text colour (an empty one was black on black on
      CRT). CRT's buttons are lighter with the same outline; flat, blueprint, vulcanus, gleba, fulgora and dark glass
      buttons are a step lighter, so a plain button doesn't read as text.
    - The theme window: themes in fixed-width cells (the third column's strips were cut on CRT), saved themes in a row
      of their own under "Your themes"; saving and sharing fold away ("Save and share", open while it holds a theme's
      text), the name field captioned, Save and Add in a plain button's size; the window may grow to 900 high. Reset
      asks with a red Reset and "Keep them".
    - Toasts go next to the mod's window (below it, past its dock; else above it, past its folder tabs; else right of
      it), never over it; at the top of the screen without one. toast{window = frame or name} picks the window. Each
      mod's toasts keep clear of the others'.
    - The library's own texts are translatable: the settings gear's and the resize grip's tooltips, a confirm's title
      and buttons ("Are you sure?", Yes, No: was Cancel), fold, search and clear, the "something went wrong" message.
    - The theme window: picking a theme or a colour updates it in place (the view no longer jumps to the top); it
      opens taller where the screen has room, so the colour's sliders show under the list; Reset sits by the colour's
      #rrggbb field and asks first when it would throw your edits away. The themes' colour strips are thicker, framed
      (they show on light themes too) and pick their theme on a click. A copy of a shipped theme without a name of its
      own is "Custom (Vanilla)" (translated), not "Vanilla (2)". "Theme copied" and an import's error are toasts.
    - Flat game windows on light themes: buttons the game draws a white icon on (the shortcut bar, the side menu's
      research and map buttons, title bar buttons) keep a dark tile, so the icon stays readable.
    - theme.confirm is as tall as its question (a long one wraps in a wider box); its buttons are never cut off.
    - theme.adopt keeps a window's size in its tweak (from the one it had, or a style.width / height it was given), so
      what is attached to it (drawers, settings) follows it outside, not inside.
    - A drop-down's arrow is light on dark themes' buttons (flat, CRT, ...).
    - "Colour the game's own windows" without flat game windows, with FlatUI's flat art: its dark panels (window,
      inset, deep) are tinted by the ratio to FlatUI's own colours, not the game's, so they come out in the theme's.
    - theme.adopt keeps a GUI's layout: each game style it themes gets its role's colours on that style itself
      (sizes, paddings, flows and fonts stay the game's), so subheaders stay 36 px strips with centred captions and
      shallow frames stay unpadded. What a mod set on an element itself (sizes, a dim text colour) is kept.
    - theme.adopt also themes subfooters, red and green buttons, flib's selected tool buttons, production progress
      bars and a title bar's close button (black on light themes). Windows themed by an older version are themed
      again as now.
    - Ticked checkboxes show a light tick on dark themes; the chosen radio button takes the accent colour.
    - Drag bars are faint: a bright bar read as a text field.
    - A caller's font colour (a tweak) wins over the theme's edited text colour.
    - theme.adopt themes all of a title bar's buttons (search, pin, settings: frame_action_button, flib's selected
      one in the accent) with dark icons on light themes; a mod's light icon without a dark one keeps the game's
      button. count_label is adopted too. Monospace themes keep the game's heading, bold and count fonts.
    - Checkbox and radio boxes have an outline, so an unticked one shows on dark themes.
    - Live colour edits reach a checkbox's tick and an adopted window's caption and title bar filler at once.
    - "Colour the game's own windows": each style is coloured by the art it is drawn with (its parents': a deep frame
      as deep, slot-sized buttons as buttons), only where it has a set of its own, and each set of the game's is
      coloured by one token only (striped rows, slot grids behind frames and the roles' own pass no longer overwrite
      each other's colours).
    - Flat game windows: frames drawn outside their area (inside_deep_frame and others) keep doing so; tiles keep the
      game's opacity, glow and shadow; selected buttons and tabs are drawn selected (tabs in their content's colour)
      and their text follows the theme; checkbox, radio button and switch text too. Only the game's plain white,
      heading and black button text changes, and only on art that was made flat: hyperlinks, warnings and
      coloured labels keep their colours. The result no longer depends on the order the styles are visited in.
    - Flat game windows and load order: a set a mod loaded after fse-std replaces again in its data-final-fixes is
      found when the game runs and coloured as the game's art, its text left alone.
