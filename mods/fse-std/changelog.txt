Version: 0.6.1
Date: 2026-10-11
  Changes:
    - theme.adopt knows more of the game's and flib's styles: subheader and heading labels (headings keep their
      font), list boxes' panes and rows, flib's title bar drag handle: they follow light themes too.

Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-10-11
  Features:
    - Theme window: a button on the shortcut bar; the themes shown as a gallery with their colours.
    - Share a theme as a line of text ("Copy as text", "Add").
    - CRT terminal and Blueprint use a monospace font (Cascadia Mono, SIL OFL 1.1).
    - Layouts for mod windows: tabs out of a window's left edge, folder tabs on its top, a toolbar hanging below.
    - With the FSE loader: line charts and glows drawn over windows; hovered buttons fade in.
    - Startup setting "Themes: flat game windows": the game's own windows in the themes' flat tiles, so light
      themes reach them too; their text follows the theme.
    - theme.adopt: a mod themes a GUI it builds with the game's styles in one call.
  Changes:
    - The close button is black on light themes.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-11
  Features:
    - Themed controls: sliders, drop-downs, scrollbars, checkboxes, radio buttons, list boxes, the game's tabs.
    - Components: progress bars, stat tiles, badges, column charts, foldable sections, search boxes, toasts, confirm
      dialogs, a pulsing main button.
    - "Colour the game's own windows" reaches every window, the quick bar, the shortcut bar, the side menu and the
      map's frame.
  Changes:
    - Colour changes are written in one batch (FSE 0.16.0): an edit with the game's windows coloured takes ~30 ms.

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-11
  Features:
    - Themes for mod windows: 12 themes (Vanilla, Compact, Flat dark, Blueprint, Space platform, Vulcanus, Gleba,
      Fulgora, Aquilo, CRT terminal, Frosted glass, Dark glass), picked per player in /fse-theme.
    - Edit a theme's colours, reset them, save as a new theme; with the FSE loader every change shows at once.
    - Frosted and Dark glass blur what is behind the window.
